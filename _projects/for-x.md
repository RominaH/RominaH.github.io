My undergraduate thesis, titled "For Woman, Life, Freedom: The BARAYEX Construction as a Discursive Practice in the 2022 Jina Amini Movement," explores how social movement discourses impact the form and meaning of constructions, and in turn, how linguistic constructions influence discourse and identity formation in social movements.
