At the moment, this project consists of two subbranches. In one branch, I investigate the use of audio as a determing factor in the form and meaning of #glowup and #throwup constructions. In another branch, I explore how social media users use trending templates to show off their pets. Ultimately, I believe in an expanded, multimodal definition of costruction, and I believe that such a definition can be helpful in studying social media and in-group dynamics.
