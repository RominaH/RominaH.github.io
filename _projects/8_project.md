At the moment, this project consists of two subbranches. In this branch, I investigate the use of audio as a determing factor in the form and meaning of <p class="small-caps">transition</p> constructions, used to share "glow ups" and "throwbacks". In another branch, I explore how social media users use trending templates to show off their pets. Ultimately, I believe in an expanded, multimodal definition of construction, and I believe that such a definition can be helpful in studying social media discourse and in-group dynamics.
