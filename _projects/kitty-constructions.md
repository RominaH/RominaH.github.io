At the moment, this project encompasses one of two subbranches of an analysis of multimodal social media constructions. In this branch, I explore how social media users use trending templates to show off their pets. In another branch, I investigate the use of audio as a determing factor in the form and meaning of #glowup and #throwback constructions. Ultimately, I believe in an expanded, multimodal definition of construction, and I believe that such a definition can be helpful in studying social media discourse and in-group dynamics.
